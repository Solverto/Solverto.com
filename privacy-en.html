Privacy policy

Information about the processing of data when using the Solverto website and contacting us by email.

Effective from 7 August 2026.

In brief

  • Contact takes place by email.
  • There is no contact form or user account system.
  • Google Analytics will operate only with consent.
  • Consent for analytics may be withdrawn at any time.

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data is Andrzej Panna, operating as a sole proprietor under the business name Solverto Andrzej Panna, hereinafter referred to as the "Controller".

For matters concerning privacy and personal data, contact us at contact@solverto.com.

2. Email contact

The website does not contain a contact form. Clicking an email address opens the user's email application; a message is provided to the Controller only after the user sends it independently.

In correspondence, we may process information supplied by the user, in particular their name, email address, company name, enquiry content and information required to respond or prepare cooperation.

The legal basis for processing is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR when the contact concerns entering into or performing a contract, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, namely the Controller's legitimate interest in handling business enquiries.

3. Purposes and retention periods

  • responding to an enquiry and conducting correspondence - for the time needed to conclude the matter, generally no longer than 12 months from the last contact;
  • entering into and performing a contract - for its duration and then until the limitation period for claims expires;
  • settlements and legal obligations - for the period required by accounting and tax regulations;
  • protection against claims and website security - for the period necessary to fulfil that purpose.

4. Recipients and data transfers

Data may be received by entities supporting the Controller in operating the website and business, in particular providers of hosting, email, IT, accounting and legal advisory services, solely to the extent necessary to provide those services. Data may also be disclosed to authorised public authorities where required by law.

If a service provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer takes place using appropriate safeguards required by the GDPR, including an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

5. Google Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics is not currently active on the website. Once implemented, it will be used solely to create aggregate visit statistics and improve the operation of the website.

Google Analytics will be activated only after voluntary consent to analytics cookies is given. Before consent is given, Google Analytics scripts will not load. The legal basis for processing analytics data will be the user's consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

After activation, the tool may process, among other things, cookie identifiers, abbreviated device and browser data, approximate location and information about how the website is used. The provider is Google LLC and entities within the Google group. Consent may be withdrawn at any time in the cookie settings available on the website.

The website also stores the selected language setting in the browser's local storage (solverto-language). This is necessary to remember the user's preference and can be removed in the browser settings.

6. User rights

The user has the right of access to their data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interest and withdrawal of consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, write to contact@solverto.com. The user also has the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website's operation, tools used or applicable law changes. The current version will always be available on this page together with its effective date.
